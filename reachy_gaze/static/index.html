Looking for . Confidence is the lowest detector score that counts. Lower snappiness is smoother but laggier. Boredom is how many seconds a still target holds its attention; it then ignores that spot for a while and looks for something else. Travel is how far it turns either way. Untick Tracking to stop and rest facing ahead; untick Sweep to have it wait where it is while searching, rather than turning side to side.
